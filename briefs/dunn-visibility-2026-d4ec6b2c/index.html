The biggest levers, in order

  1. Claim & verify both Google Business Profiles highest impact

    If nobody's managing them, this is the single fastest win — it unlocks reviews, photos, categories, and the "call" button that drives the local map results.

  2. Turn on a steady review engine

    Going from 6 → 50+ and 14 → 50+ reviews is the biggest driver of where you rank in the map pack. A simple after-job ask, made routine, compounds fast.

  3. Fix the name & address conflicts

    Advanced's profile says Fort Mill while the site says Indian Land, and both brands share one building. Google needs one clean, consistent story per business to trust and rank either.

  4. Add "local business" markup + fix the category

    Both sites tell Google they're a generic "organization," not a local contractor with services and a service area. A quick, behind-the-scenes upgrade that helps in search and in AI answers.

  5. Clean up the pages that undercut trust

    Advanced has placeholder "Lorem Ipsum" text still live on two pages and several thin pages; both sites carry heavy, uncompressed images that slow phones down. Small fixes, real polish.

  6. Build service-area pages for the searches buyers type

    "Deck builder in Fort Mill," "kitchen remodel Indian Land" — you have the services, just not the pages that capture those geo-specific searches.
