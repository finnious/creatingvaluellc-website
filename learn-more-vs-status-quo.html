Your systems already hold the answer. It's just trapped.

"It works fine" is doing a lot of heavy lifting.

The business runs. Orders go out, shelves get stocked, reports get sent. But look closely at how, and you find people quietly holding the whole thing together by hand: re-keying numbers from one system into another, hunting for the current version of a report, sorting through inbox floods for the two emails that actually apply to them. That manual effort is a cost you're already paying — you just don't get an invoice for it.

And underneath the manual effort sits risk you can't see until it bites:

  • Scheduled jobs that have been failing for weeks before anyone notices a number looks wrong
  • Stale data — up to 23 days old in one case — going out as if it were current
  • Ghost recipients — former employees still on daily reports, or still holding access. In one engagement, someone was still verifying timecards in payroll after they'd left.
  • Cost and margin visible to people who stand in front of customers

From exhaust to signal

Your systems already produce everything you need to run tighter — it's just coming out as exhaust. We build the pipeline from raw data to the specific person who acts on it:

  • One nightly export (31 columns, 57,558 rows) → a purchasing report opened at 6 AM and acted on
  • The same data → bin-sorted walk-lists for stockers, store-tour sheets for managers, a customer-safe price-only view for the floor
  • 12 drifting report versions → 1 clean daily pipeline
  • ~750–800 unnecessary weekly emails → routed only to the people who act
