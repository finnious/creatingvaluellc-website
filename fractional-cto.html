The problem

Growth quietly outgrows your systems.

You added tools as you grew, but nobody's job is to make them work together. So people hold it together by hand — and the gaps stay invisible until they bite.

✕

The same report exists in several drifting versions — nobody's sure which is current.

✕

Staff re-key and reformat data between systems by hand, every day.

✕

Information floods every inbox instead of reaching the people who act on it.

✕

Scheduled jobs fail silently for weeks; stale data goes out as current.

What changes

From exhaust to signal.

Before

  • 12 versions of one report, drifting
  • One form submission hits 50–65 inboxes
  • Raw exports nobody opens
  • Broken jobs no one is watching

After

  • One clean daily pipeline — one source of truth
  • The right info reaches only who acts on it
  • Each role gets data in the shape it can use
  • Failures surfaced and fixed; fresh data daily

Frequently asked

Who is this for?

Established B2B businesses (roughly $5M–$50M, 5–100 people) on Google Workspace, with real people doing real work across locations or departments — and tools but no IT team making them work together.

How fast do we see something real?

First useful deliverables land in week one. The Systems Snapshot itself is about 20 minutes and gives you a concrete fix to consider.

Do you keep our client's name confidential?

Always. Our proof comes from a real engagement and is fully anonymized — no client, staff, vendor, or system names. Your work would be treated the same way.

Do we own what you build?

Yes. Everything runs on the tools you already pay for, documented and organized so your team maintains it. No lock-in, no dependency on us.

What does it cost?

The Systems Snapshot is free. The First Fix is a flat $500 — one working build, live in a week, so we can both see the fit. Beyond that, work is scoped per engagement — project or retainer.
